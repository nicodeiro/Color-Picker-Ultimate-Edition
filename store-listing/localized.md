## English (`en`)

Color Picker Ultimate Edition lets you instantly pick any color visible on your screen and get ready-to-use values in multiple formats.

Key features:

• Precise eyedropper powered by Chrome
• Instant HEX, RGB and HSL values
• One-click copy to the clipboard
• Local history of recently picked colors
• Favorite colors for quick access
• Light, dark or system theme
• Interface available in eight languages
• No user account required

How to use:

1. Open Color Picker from the Chrome toolbar.
2. Click the eyedropper button.
3. Select any visible color on screen.
4. View and copy its HEX, RGB or HSL value.
5. Find the color later in your history or favorites.

Selected colors, history, favorites and preferences are stored locally on the user’s device with Chrome storage. Selected colors are not sent to a server, and the extension does not execute remote code.

Color Picker Ultimate Edition is designed for developers, designers, content creators and anyone who needs to identify a color quickly.
